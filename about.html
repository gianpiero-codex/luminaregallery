About

Decorative wall art for real homes.

Luminare Gallery creates accessible decorative wall art for rooms people actually live in: kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, coffee corners, prayer spaces, and everyday walls that need a clear visual touch.

The focus is readable, warm, commercial home decor. Designs are made to be easy to understand at a glance, simple to style, and suitable for modern homes and giftable decor.

Products are available through Etsy. This website is a brand and catalog website, not a direct checkout store, so customers can browse here and complete purchases through the Etsy shop or individual Etsy listings.
