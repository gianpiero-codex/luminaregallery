LuminareGallery TikTok Integration

LuminareGallery is a private internal tool used by the shop owner to prepare and upload short-form promotional videos for Luminare Gallery wall art products to TikTok.

The integration is intended only for authorized use by the Luminare Gallery owner. It supports manual content preparation, TikTok account authorization, video selection, caption preparation, and upload through TikTok developer tools.

This service does not scrape TikTok data, does not automate spam, and does not publish content on behalf of third-party users.
