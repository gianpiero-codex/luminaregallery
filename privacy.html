Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LuminareGallery handles information in connection with its private internal TikTok integration.

Purpose of the Tool

LuminareGallery is a private internal tool used by the Luminare Gallery owner to prepare and upload short-form promotional videos related to Luminare Gallery wall art products.

Information We May Process

The tool may process information required to connect with TikTok developer services, including TikTok authorization data, account identifiers provided by TikTok, selected video files, captions, upload status, and technical logs required to complete the upload process.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authorize the owner’s TikTok account, prepare selected content, upload videos to TikTok, and confirm upload status. The tool is not used to collect data from third-party TikTok users.

Data Sharing

Information is shared only with TikTok as necessary to complete the requested authorization and video upload process. LuminareGallery does not sell personal data and does not share data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data Storage

Any authorization or technical data is used only for the operation of the internal tool. Access tokens or related credentials should be stored securely and only for as long as necessary to operate the integration.

User Control

The authorized TikTok account owner may revoke access through TikTok account settings or by discontinuing use of the tool.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the tool. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: luminare.gallery@gmail.com
